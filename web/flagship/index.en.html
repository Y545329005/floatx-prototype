Risk controls

Capital safety

Risk disclosure and important notices

Risk of loss ESSENTIAL

Private equity investments may result in partial or total loss of capital due to business failure, market changes or constrained exit routes. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

Liquidity and tenor

Interests in private funds have no liquid market and are typically locked up for several years; early exit may not be possible.

Dilution and distribution

Subsequent financings may dilute your interests; distributions depend on actual performance and related decisions and are not guaranteed.

Reduced regulatory protections

Once treated as a Professional Investor, certain regulatory protections applicable to retail investors will not apply, including some suitability assessments, disclosure requirements and cooling-off arrangements.

No virtual asset trading

The Platform provides private equity information and advisory services only and does not involve any virtual asset trading or the promotion of any related product.
