VI. How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have been informed of our operations regarding the use of Cookies and agree to authorize us to use any data collected by us through the use of Cookies for the purposes set out in this Policy Statement.

1. What are Cookies

Cookies are a string of plain text data. When you use the Platform services, we will assign one or more small data files named Cookies to you based on your usage. Such data may include your encrypted login information, previously browsed pages, and browsing time and date, so that the Platform can know your identity and remember your preferences when you browse again, thereby providing you with a better user experience.

For example: When you visit information or services that require you to log in, when you log in, we will encrypt and store your login name in the Cookies file of your computer or device terminal. Since it is an irreversible encrypted storage, even if others can use your computer or device terminal, they cannot identify your login name. You do not need to do any extra work; all collection, saving, and encryption work is automatically completed by the system.

2. Saving and Deleting Cookies Files

Cookies files will be saved on your computer hard drive or the flash memory of your device terminal. They can only be read by the server that sets them and cannot execute any code or viruses. You can delete them at any time using your browser or operating system software.

3. How We Use Cookies and Similar Technologies

To provide you with more convenient, fast, smooth, and personalized services, we provide you with the following services through technologies such as Cookies:

(1) Remember your account. For example, Cookies may help you skip the step of repeatedly entering your account to use the Platform services.

(2) Analyze your use of the Platform services. For example, we can use technologies such as Cookies and web beacons to understand the specific purposes for which you use the Platform services, or which web pages or services are most popular with you.

(3) Advertising optimization. We may display products or advertising information that you may be interested in based on the statistical information of your visits to relevant websites. These statistical information do not include any of your personal information, and these information may be used to evaluate the effectiveness of advertisements. Technologies such as Cookies and web beacons help us provide you with advertisements relevant to you based on your information rather than general advertising.

(4) We may also use web beacon technology to count anonymous access data. Similarly, these statistical information do not include your personal information.

(5) Cookies and web beacon technologies may also be used by our third-party partners. The collection and use of such information by these third parties through Cookies and web beacons are not governed by this Policy Statement, but are governed by the privacy policies of such third parties. We recommend that before you use the services provided by third parties through Cookies and web beacons, you should carefully read and decide for yourself whether to be bound by the privacy policies of such third parties.

4. Refusing Our Use of Cookies

Generally speaking, your initial setting for browsing the Platform is to accept Cookies. However, you have various tools to control the data collected by Cookies. For example, you can disable Cookies by changing the internet software browsing settings. You may also set in the browser settings to accept specific Cookies or be notified every time new Cookies are about to be stored in your computer/device terminal, so that you can decide to accept or reject the relevant Cookies.

Given that our Cookies allow you to use some services of the Platform, it is recommended that you keep Cookies enabled. Once Cookies are disabled, the services you can experience may be reduced, or you may be completely unable to use the Platform services.
